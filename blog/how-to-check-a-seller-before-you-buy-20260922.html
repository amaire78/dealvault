How to Check a Seller Before You Buy

2026-09-22

Why the seller matters

On marketplaces, the same product can be sold by many different sellers. The price may be similar, but the experience of buying from them can be very different. A few minutes of checking can save a lot of trouble.

What to look at

  • Feedback score and percentage: Look for a long history of positive feedback, not just a high percentage from a handful of sales.
  • Recent feedback: Read the latest comments. A seller who was good last year may have problems now.
  • Negative feedback details: Look for patterns such as items not as described, slow shipping, or poor communication.
  • Listing quality: Real photos of the actual item, a clear condition description, and specific details are good signs. Stock photos only, especially on used items, deserve caution.
  • Location and shipping time: Know where the item ships from and how long it will take.
  • Return policy: A seller who offers returns is standing behind the product.

Red flags

  • A price far below every other seller for the same new item.
  • Requests to pay or communicate outside the marketplace.
  • Vague descriptions that avoid stating the condition.
  • A brand-new seller account offering high-value items at deep discounts.

Use the marketplace protections

Always pay through the marketplace's own checkout so its buyer protection applies. Keep your order confirmation and communicate through the platform's messaging system.

The bottom line

A strong discount from a trustworthy seller is worth far more than a slightly bigger discount from one you can't trust. Check the seller as carefully as you check the price.

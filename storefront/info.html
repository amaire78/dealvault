About DealVault

Most deal sites bury visitors in hundreds of listings a day. DealVault is building a smaller, curated shopping resource supported by practical guides about evaluating online discounts.

How We Verify Discounts

A seller can list any "was" price they like, so we do not take those at face value. Before a listing appears here, we compare the claimed original price against recent sold-price history for the same or comparable item, and against pricing for that product elsewhere.

Examples that clear our review threshold may be displayed to demonstrate the planned service. Retailer links remain disabled before approval. Once links are activated, visitors should always confirm the final price and availability on the retailer's page.
